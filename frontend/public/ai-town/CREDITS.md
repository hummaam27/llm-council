# Sandbox art credits

The pixel-art tileset, town map, and character sprites used by the Sandbox
mode were sourced from the open-source **AI Town** project by a16z-infra
(MIT licensed): https://github.com/a16z-infra/ai-town

AI Town in turn credits these original artists:

- Tilesets — George Bailey and "hilau" on OpenGameArt
  (https://opengameart.org/content/16x16-game-assets,
   https://opengameart.org/content/16x16-rpg-tileset)
- Original environment assets by **ansimuz** — "Tiny RPG Forest"
  (https://opengameart.org/content/tiny-rpg-forest)

Files in this folder (`rpg-tileset.png`, `32x32folk.png`) and the derived
`town.json` (a 15x15 crop of AI Town's map) are used here for a
non-commercial portfolio project, with attribution as required.
